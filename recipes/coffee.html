Coffee


Description

There is nothing more refreshing than a cup of coffee. Who doesn't like a cup of coffee at down time?. It is easy to make and have at any point of the day.

Let us check what all the ingredients are and how to prepare it in 5 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 Teaspoon coffee powder
  • 1 Teaspoon sugar
  • 250 ml of Milk
  • 1 Glass of water

Steps

  1. Add 1 glass of water to 250ml milk and start boiling.
  2. When the boiling is completed, add a teaspoon each of coffee powder and sugar to the container.
  3. Mix it well and you have a delicious cup of coffee.
