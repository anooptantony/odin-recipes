Omelette


Description

Are you looking for a good protein rich Omelette dish that can be prepared in no time? Well here it is! A fresh omelette.

Here we explain what the ingredients are and how to prepare it in 5 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • Half teaspoon salt
  • Half teaspoon pepper
  • 1 Teaspoon coconut oil

Steps

  1. Add 1 teaspoon coconut oil to a fry pan and start the burner. Wait for 1 minute.
  2. Add two broken whole eggs to it and wait for two minutes.
  3. Put half teaspoon each of salt and pepper. Wait for another minute and your dish is ready.
